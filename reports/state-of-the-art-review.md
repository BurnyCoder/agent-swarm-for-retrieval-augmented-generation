# State of the art and comparison limits

The closest code task is [SWE-QA](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.402/). Its tables compare static retrieval, SWE-agent and OpenHands at multiple backbones. [DeepRepoQA](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.24221) adds tree-guided search and evidence verification. [The direct comparison of delegated grep and semantic search](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.01507) reports a different ordering on its configuration. Together these studies motivate both an unmodified shell agent and a retrieval-only control. Different question versions, judges, backbone models and output limits prevent a pooled leaderboard.

For documents, [MultiHop-RAG](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.15391v1) supplies short labels and article evidence. [QCG-RAG](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.21237) evaluates query-centric graphs on a subset. [RISE](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.06880) compares retriever-only, shell and navigable workspaces on a different corpus, BrowseComp-Plus. Its results motivate a mechanism to test here. They do not establish a Luna result for local news.

The relevant local comparator is the [preregistered Arctic study](https://github.com/BurnyCoder/arcticswarm-folder-rag-research/blob/bd5b3ed98cde10e9c1f90795f2182d84ecd51ddc/reports/preregistration.md). The earlier Haiku results serve as a historical bridge. The causal comparisons in the present work use the same Luna API model, corpus, time and token ceilings. Potential explanations for gains include stronger retrieval, parallel search diversity and better evidence synthesis. The prespecified ablations distinguish these accounts.

Current product capabilities were checked against [Codex non-interactive execution](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/non-interactive-mode), [local MCP servers](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/extend/mcp?surface=cli) and [Luna function calling and rates](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna). The private endpoint catalogue listed the requested model. The preregistration predates paid inference on the new system.
